NCTB Exam Predictor

A syllabus-aligned study assistant for Bangladesh NCTB learners. It helps turn the textbook, teacher emphasis, class notes and comparable past papers into a prioritized revision plan and useful practice questions.

Use in ChatGPTGet support

Start with your materials

Use a textbook, chapter list, teacher-marked pages, notes, guidebook extracts or previous school papers. A guidebook is optional.

Practice in your format

Request MCQs, short answers, creative questions where applicable, answer keys or a mock paper. The assistant checks the style sample before building a full bank unless you skip that step.

Understand topic chances

When enough comparable historical papers are available, the assistant shows an explicitly labelled historical-baseline estimate and sample size. Evidence priority is shown separately.

Important: This is an independent study aid, not an official NCTB product. Estimates are not guarantees and do not predict the exact wording of a future exam. Always cover the required syllabus and verify current rules with your teacher or official materials.
