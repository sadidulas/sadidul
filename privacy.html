Privacy Policy

This notice explains what information may be involved when you visit the NCTB Exam Predictor information pages or use the NCTB Exam Predictor prompt-based study assistant created by Sadidul Mehal.

In brief: The project is a skills/instructions-based assistant used within ChatGPT. The creator does not operate a separate NCTB Exam Predictor account system, payment processor or database for the skill. ChatGPT and its available features process the messages and files you choose to submit under the platform's own policies and settings.

1. Information you choose to provide

When using the assistant, you may choose to share your class, subject, chapter, exam format, notes, textbook excerpts, teacher markings, guidebook pages or previous question papers. These materials may be included in your ChatGPT conversation. Do not upload passwords, government identification numbers, private contact details, or unrelated information about yourself or other people. Redact names and identifying details from school papers where possible.

2. Who processes the information

Your conversation and uploads are handled by ChatGPT/OpenAI and any platform features you choose to use, according to the applicable OpenAI Privacy Policy and your account's data controls. The assistant's instructions do not give the creator an independent feed of your ChatGPT conversations or uploaded files. If you independently contact the creator by email, WhatsApp or Instagram, that service will handle the message and related account or technical data under its own policies.

3. Website technical data

The website pages are static information pages and do not ask you to create an account or submit a form. The hosting provider and any services required to deliver the site may process technical request information, such as basic connection and browser data, for delivery, reliability and security under their own terms. External links take you to third-party services.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

The creator does not maintain a separate database for the prompt-based assistant. The creator cannot independently access or delete the copy of a conversation or upload held by ChatGPT. Use the platform's available chat, file and privacy controls for those records. Messages sent directly to the creator may remain in the recipient's mailbox or third-party messaging service until deleted according to that service's operation and policies.

5. Children and school materials

Use the assistant only if you meet the age and permission requirements that apply to your ChatGPT account. Follow your school’s rules and obtain permission before sharing materials that belong to others. The creator does not ask users to provide sensitive student records.

6. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the website or assistant's operation changes. The effective date above indicates the latest stated revision. For privacy questions about the project, email sadidul2012@gmail.com. For data processed by ChatGPT or other third-party services, contact the relevant provider directly.
