1. Intended use

Use the assistant to support legitimate studying, syllabus review, practice question generation and revision planning. Do not use it to seek leaked or confidential exam papers, bypass exam security, or violate your school's academic integrity rules.

2. Educational accuracy and limitations

The assistant may analyze materials you provide and publicly available sources when those capabilities are available. Generated questions, summaries and topic estimates can contain errors or omissions. A topic's historical appearance estimate is not a guarantee, does not predict the exact wording of a future paper, and depends on the comparability and quality of the papers reviewed. Verify important information against the current textbook, syllabus, teacher instructions and official sources. Do not skip required material solely because a topic receives a lower priority score.

5. Materials and responsible use

Share only material you have the right to use. Respect copyright and privacy when uploading textbook pages, teacher notes, guidebook extracts and previous papers. Avoid sharing personal, confidential or identifying information about other students. Generated practice questions should not be represented as authentic past-paper questions unless their source has been verified.
